# Code of conduct

Helix Community is a small public bench for a **paper-only** research desk. We need more people, not a thicker rulebook. This is the light version.

## Expected

- **Be civil.** Disagreement is fine; contempt is not.
- **Be specific.** Quote the doc, the port, the fixture. Vague pile-ons waste the queue.
- **Assume good faith** until someone pastes a secret or a slur.
- **Keep it research.** Critique methods, not people’s net worth.
- **Protect secrets.** Yours and everyone else’s. No keys in issues, PRs, or screenshots.
- **Paper-only framing.** Do not coach live orders. Do not present Helix as a broker or as advice.

## Not welcome

- Harassment, hate, or targeted personal attacks
- Doxxing, scraping private data, or “OSINT” that is just stalking
- Pasting credentials, cookies, seeds, or customer data
- Pump language, guaranteed returns, or “this desk says buy”
- Copying the private helix-cx tree into this public repo
- Spam, drive-by promo, and vendor astroturf

## Paper-desk extra

Helix talks about markets. That is not a license to:

- Pressure anyone to fund an account
- Mock people for missing a move
- Treat DARK data as a dare to hack a vendor

If a thread turns into live-trading cheerleading, maintainers will close it and point at [docs/CONCEPT.md](docs/CONCEPT.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, edit, lock, or ban. For a leaked secret, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) immediately — etiquette is second.

Report conduct problems to the maintainers via GitHub (private contact if the platform offers it). Do not escalate by quoting the worst of a thread in a new public issue.
